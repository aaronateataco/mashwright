Hue Potato bundles one third-party tool, unmodified: vgmstream r2117 (Windows x64 build from
https://github.com/vgmstream/vgmstream/releases/tag/r2117). The mod uses it at runtime to turn the
audio in the player's own copy of Hue (FMOD FSB5 / Vorbis) into sound Brotato can play. No Hue or
Brotato files are included with the mod.
