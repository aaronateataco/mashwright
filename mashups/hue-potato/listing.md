**Hue joins Brotato.** Pick *Hue Potato* on the character select: a dark potato wearing Hue's own hair, ear and eyes, carrying Hue's colour wheel.

### Hue's colour wheel
Hold **Right Click / Space / a trigger** and time slows to Hue's own 0.1×. Pick a colour and the arena takes it on — and **every enemy and enemy bullet of that colour vanishes**. Hidden enemies can't hurt you, but you can't hurt them either: switch back to bring them into range. Quick switch with **Q / E** (or the mouse wheel / bumpers); **G** goes back to grey.

### Hue's Laser Ring
Hue Potato's own weapon: Hue's laser cannons orbit you, each firing Hue's laser beam at the next, making a spinning fence that closes in and opens out to zap enemies near and far. The beams take the colours you've found — and a beam the colour of the background vanishes, leaving a gap, just like Hue's lasers. Upgrades add cannons: a triangle, a square, a pentagon, a hexagon.

### Earn the colours like in Hue
You start with Aqua and Purple. A colour fragment appears every other wave — Orange, Pink, Red, Blue, Yellow, Lime — and enemies only come in colours you've found. Bosses, elites and loot aliens stay solid, like Hue's black objects.

### Mum's letters
Mum's intro letter opens the run; every region and every fragment brings her letter for it; win the run and she reads her last letter and PS. All in her own voice from Hue, with Hue's subtitles in your language (English, French, Russian, German or Spanish).

### Hue's obstacles
From wave 3, **laser gates** switch on: two of Hue's laser cannons at the arena's edges with a laser wall between them. From wave 7, **skull boulders** roll at you. Switch to their colour and they vanish — walk through the laser, let the boulder roll through you. Brotato's trees become **Hue's coloured crates**, and **Dr Grey** appears for the final wave with his music from Hue.

### A run through Hue's world
Waves travel through the Village, the Waterfall, the Fire Caves, the Temple, the Mountains, the Laboratory, the University and the Island — each with Hue's map icon and Hue's music.

### Hue items in the shop
Mum's Letter, Hue's Beaker, Mum's Goggles, Grey Butterfly, Village Lantern and Lighthouse Life Ring, with Hue's art as icons. Several change how the wheel plays (heal on a fragment, materials or slows when you reveal enemies, heal on a switch).

### You need
- **Brotato** (Steam, 1.1.15 or newer)
- **Hue** installed (Steam; GOG/Epic copies are found too). Everything from Hue — art, music, sounds and Mum's letters — is read from **your own copy** while you play; nothing from Hue is included. Without Hue the title screen tells you, and Hue Potato isn't offered.

Single player only — no online multiplayer.
